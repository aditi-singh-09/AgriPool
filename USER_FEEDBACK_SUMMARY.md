# 👥 User Feedback Summary

We successfully onboarded **11 real users** to the AgriPool platform during our testing phase. We collected their feedback using a Google Form to validate our product and shape future development.

**🔗 Raw Data Link**: [View Google Sheet Data](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1VBYFa_4_aT5cyX14fPrrb75yxhFK0no4KCQdr3iuO50/edit?usp=sharing)
**🔗 Collection Form**: [View Google Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScrNl9nkIwUQDFhyBOCX7vWdJeD8Tt_rTe-ZFS_5-bCGTc48A/viewform?usp=dialog)

## 📊 Overview
- **Total Users Surveyed**: 11
- **Average Overall Experience Rating**: 4.2/5.0

## 🎯 Key Themes & Insights

1. **User Interface & Experience**
   Users consistently praised the clean UI and smooth wallet connection via Freighter. The decentralized, middleman-free architecture was highlighted as a major positive factor in establishing trust.
   
2. **Feature Requests**
   Several key suggestions emerged from the early testing phase:
   - Better marketplace filtering (distance/region sorting).
   - Real-time market price estimation tools to assist farmers.
   - Total seasonal earnings tracker on the cooperative dashboard.
   - Quality inspection certificate uploads for trust verification.
   
3. **Platform Enhancements**
   Users asked for small quality-of-life additions, such as crop alerts, grouping small harvests into single listings, and in-app chat for logistics discussions.
